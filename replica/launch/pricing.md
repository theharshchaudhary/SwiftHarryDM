# Pricing

**Keepfetch is free.** No tiers, no trial, no licence key, no Stripe. That is the point of
the angle (`fixes.md`, option A), so there are no products or prices to create.

## Billing complaints to fix in the product

None apply: there is no billing. What does apply:
- **No nag:** no "register", "upgrade" or trial dialogs. Licensing code was removed in
  0f68c5a.
- **No silent cost:** updates are free and signed.

## If money is wanted later

- Donations (GitHub Sponsors or similar), with no features behind them. Your decision,
  your account.
- Never a "Pro" that takes back a feature from this list: that would break the angle.
